## 3. Historical Price Range & Walk-Forward Coverage (`paddy.csv`)

### Data Quality Note (`min` / `max` columns)
`backend/data/prices/paddy.csv` contains 61 monthly rows (`2020-10-01` to `2025-10-01`) from CEDA Agmarknet. In recent months, `min` and `max` are inconsistent (with `max` below `modal`, e.g., `2025-10-01` has `modal=2296.75`, `min=661.06`, `max=721.74`). CropIQ therefore uses the **`modal` column only** and ignores `min` and `max`.

### Why No ML Price Forecast Is Shown
On the 61-month Karnataka paddy modal series, 4-month-ahead Random Forest and Ridge regression forecasts did not beat the naive persistence baseline ("price stays at today's modal value"). Therefore, CropIQ never displays an ML price forecast; instead it reports a **historical 4-month 5th–95th percentile log-change band** (`latest_modal * exp(quantile(ln(p[t+4]/p[t]), q))`), labelled in the UI as a *"Typical historical range, not a guarantee."*
